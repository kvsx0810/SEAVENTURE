# 'Embrace' generative art
Version 1.0 — August 2026

## Overview
This project is a digital media artwork created with p5.js that visualizes the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 14: Life Below Water. Specifically SDG target 14.2: Protect and Restore Ecosystems. 

---

## About

'Embrace' explores the relationship between humans and marine ecosystems through an interactive underwater scene.
The experience combines generative animation, holographic forms, marine life, ambient sound, and visual elements to create a small underwater world that feels alive and interconnected.
The central globe represents a marine ecosystem, surrounded by a holographic "embrace" that symbolizes human protection and care for the ocean.

---

## Concept

The project is inspired by **Sustainable Development Goal 14.2**, which focuses on sustainably managing and protecting marine and coastal ecosystems.
Rather than presenting the ocean as a resource to be harvested, 'Embrace' encourages viewers to be aware that protection is a cycle, protecting the ocean is also protecting human kind.

---

## Installation Instructions

- Click anywhere on the canvas to start the ambient soundscape.
- The visual elements continue to animate automatically, while the fish move dynamically within the central globe.

---

## Sound

The experience uses four recorded ambient sounds:

- 'clothes_hook.wav'
- 'wind_chime.wav'
- 'water_splash.wav'
- 'water_drop.wav'

The sounds play together as a layered ambient soundscape, with the wind chime intentionally mixed louder than the other sounds, creating a peaceful atmosphere, representing a better outsome if the ocean is protected.

Because browsers restrict websites from automatically playing audio, the visitor needs to click the canvas once to activate the sound.
