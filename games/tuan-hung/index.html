Abstract

"Last Catch" is one piece inside SEAVENTURE, a shared arcade cabinet built around SDG 14: Life Below Water. The player controls a boat, deciding when and where to fish a reef that only gets worse the more it's taken from.

SDG 14.4

This game is inspired by SDG target 14.4: "By 2020, effectively regulate harvesting and end overfishing, illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing and destructive fishing practices..."

Catching a fish before it has had the chance to reproduce costs the ecosystem more than catching one that already has.

Goal

The game asks players to weigh short-term gain against long-term cost, one cast at a time.
